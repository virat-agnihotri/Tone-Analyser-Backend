High stress levels during wheel-to-wheel battles increase muscle tension and cause late braking, resulting in lockups and compromised exit speed.
Controlled tactical radio communications keep driver heart rate low. Short, precise messages improve focus and pit strategy execution.
When tire temperatures spike, early throttle application causes snap oversteer. Smooth inputs stabilize the chassis on corner exit.
In qualifying laps under pressure, focus on trail braking into hairpins to maintain minimum cornering speed without destabilizing the rear axle.
High vocal pitch and rapid speaking rate indicate elevated stress. Race engineers should deliver calming, direct telemetry feedback to lower driver anxiety.
